Typography. A 19th-c. AR / glass-specific cautions

  • The phone camera is the "magic window": the feed is world-locked by physics, so it must stay the only stable world anchor. Anything the HUD adds that doesn't move with the world = conflict.
  • No parallax in a 2D phone HUD (no head tracking): a parallax expectation with no depth reality is the nausea recipe. Use tonal contrast + scale for depth.
  • Head-locked ≠ 1:1. AHFE HIG 2022: content should loosely follow; large content must not be head-locked. Motion-to-photon < 300 ms; sickness scales with angular velocity.
  • Re-anchoring here is semantic, not spatial: "which stop / how far / which way" answerable in one glance, and every indicator must agree.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion, prefers-contrast: more, prefers-reduced-transparency. Never backdrop-filter over #cam, no contrast ratio can be computed against a background that changes 30×/second.

Highest risks of the redesign

  1. Contrast over arbitrary video. Beautiful on a dark mock, unreadable on white pavement at noon. Test every scheme against worst-case frames (white sky, white wall, sunlit asphalt).
